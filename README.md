
# 🩺 MediCase AI
### Intelligent Clinical & Patient Workspace

**AI-assisted case-taking. Smarter triage. Better-connected healthcare.**

MediCase AI is a healthcare technology project designed to streamline patient case-taking, support clinical prioritization, and make healthcare information more accessible through AI-assisted workflows.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://medi-case.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 About the Project

Patient case-taking in hospitals and clinics can be time-consuming. Important symptoms, medical history, allergies, and previous prescriptions may be difficult to collect and organize efficiently.

MediCase AI aims to simplify this process through a unified workspace for doctors and patients, combining digital case intake, multilingual communication, medical document processing, and AI-assisted clinical support.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚨 1. AI Smart Triage
- Organizes cases into P1 Emergency, P2 Urgent, and P3 Routine categories.
- Helps clinical staff identify cases requiring prompt attention.
- Supports red-flag identification and urgency assessment.

### 📝 2. Digital Case-Taking
- Collects patient demographics and chief complaints.
- Captures symptom duration, medical history, current medications, and allergies.
- Supports structured clinical documentation.

### 🎙️ 3. Voice-Assisted Input
- Voice-to-text input for recording symptoms and clinical notes.
- Designed to reduce manual typing during case intake.
- Supports English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam.

### 📄 4. Medical Document Processing
- Medical report and prescription upload interface.
- OCR-assisted extraction of clinical information.
- Designed to organize important findings for clinical review.

### 🤖 5. AI Clinical Assistance
- AI-assisted clinical summaries and missing-field checks.
- Preliminary first-aid information for patients.
- Red-flag alerts and structured information to support doctor review.

### 👨‍⚕️ 6. Dedicated Doctor Portal
- Clinical dashboard and triage queue.
- New case intake and patient registry.
- AI assistance for clinical workflows.

### 👤 7. Patient Portal
- Submit symptoms before consultation.
- Access prescription and health-history sections.
- View preliminary first-aid guidance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Reduce repetitive manual work during patient intake.
- Improve the organization of clinical information.
- Support timely identification of potentially urgent cases.
- Make symptom reporting more accessible through multilingual input.
- Keep healthcare professionals involved in clinical decisions.

## 🛡️ Safety & Medical Disclaimer

MediCase AI is an AI-assisted healthcare project intended to support information collection and clinical workflows. It is not a replacement for qualified healthcare professionals.

AI-generated summaries, triage suggestions, and preliminary guidance require appropriate validation. The application must not be relied upon as the sole basis for diagnosis, treatment, or emergency decisions.

Do not upload real patient information to an unverified demo environment.
